# VisionFX Content Clarity Plan

The visual layout is approved and must not be materially changed. This pass rewrites the copy so a prospective client can answer four questions in the opening minute: **Is this for a business like mine? What does VisionFX do? What will I receive? How do I start?**

| Current pattern | Problem for a prospective client | Rewrite rule |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract phrases such as “make the next move clear” | Sounds polished but does not identify the service or client situation quickly enough. | Pair the positioning line with a concrete sentence about websites for growing businesses, redesigns, launches, and new offers. |
| “Project directions” and representative engagement language | Can sound like a portfolio, a concept board, or a template catalog. | State that these are the types of website projects VisionFX takes on, then list a clear outcome and deliverables. |
| Capability labels without a clear sequence | A visitor may not know whether services are strategy, design, development, or all three. | Name the three services in plain verbs: plan the website, design the interface, build and launch it. |
| “Start a conversation” only | It does not set an expectation for what happens next. | Use a direct inquiry action and explain in the contact copy that VisionFX will respond with scope, timing, and a practical next step. |

The style stays concise and editorial, but every section must lead with a concrete sentence before any brand-language flourish.
